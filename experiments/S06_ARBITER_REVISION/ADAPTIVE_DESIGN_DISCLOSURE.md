# S06 Adaptive Design Disclosure

**Author:** Eng. Mohammed Qaid Alathwry
**Date:** 2026-10-29

## Purpose
This document explicitly discloses the historical sequence of the S06 experimental phases. 

## Sequence of Events
1. **S06a (Exploratory):** The v1 and v2 models were run and evaluated.
2. **S06b/S06c (Confirmatory):** The v3 and v4 models, along with the confirmatory protocol, were designed **after** observing the exploratory results of S06a.

## Methodological Position
Because the confirmatory protocols for v3 and v4 were designed with knowledge of the v1/v2 results, S06b and S06c are classified as **adaptive/exploratory** rather than purely pre-registered confirmatory studies. I disclose this to ensure complete transparency regarding the timeline of my hypothesis generation and testing.
